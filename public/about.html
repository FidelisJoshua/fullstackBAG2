About the project

A public record kept by the people who live here

Anyone in OgoniLand can report an oill spill, a gas flare, polluted water or dumped waste. Every report is public and HYPREP follow each report until it is resolved and closed

How a report moves

1

You Report it

Create an account with your phone number, login and report it by describing what you saw, where and when

2

HYPREP reviews it

The HYPREP team checks and reviews every report and visits the site when needed

3

Everyone can follow it up

The status and HYPREP's remark are public even when it is closed

What each status mean

New Recieved, but not yet reviewed
Under review Recieved, and it is being reviewed
Resolved Recieved, and it has been resolved
Not an incident Checked and it is not an incident

HYPREP staff: set statuses on the staff admin page
